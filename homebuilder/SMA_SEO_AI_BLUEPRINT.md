# SMA Builders — SEO + AI Search Blueprint

## Objective
Build a long-term organic search and AI-search presence for SMA Builders around genuine construction expertise, real projects, local relevance and useful content. No ranking guarantees or manipulative tactics.

## Core page map
- Home — broad brand + construction company intent
- About — company/entity trust and history
- Residential Construction — commercial-intent service page
- Commercial Construction — commercial-intent service page
- Grey Structure Construction — service + informational intent
- Turnkey Construction — service intent
- Renovation & Remodeling — service intent
- Interior & Exterior Finishing — service intent
- Projects — portfolio/entity evidence
- Knowledge Center — topical authority
- Blog — supporting informational content
- Contact — conversion

4. Renovation → planning + scope + structural changes + finishing + budgeting
5. Materials → cement + steel + bricks + concrete + waterproofing + electrical/plumbing planning

## AI-search content pattern
For important guides: direct answer first → detailed explanation → local context → genuine SMA Builders experience → FAQs → related internal links → appropriate CTA.

## Internal linking rules
Service pages link to relevant projects and guides. Guides link back to the service page when commercial intent is relevant. Project pages link to the services actually delivered. Avoid artificial or repetitive anchor text.

## Technical SEO baseline
HTTPS, crawlable HTML, XML sitemap, robots.txt, canonical URLs, unique titles/meta descriptions, one clear H1, logical headings, responsive design, optimized images, valid status codes, redirects when URLs change, structured data where appropriate, Search Console and analytics after launch.

## AI/GEO principles
Make entity identity, location, services, experience and project evidence explicit. Prefer original first-hand information over generic AI copy. Do not fabricate facts, reviews, prices, certifications or projects.

## Measurement
Track Search Console queries/impressions/clicks/CTR, indexed pages, organic leads, local visibility, content performance and AI-search visibility checks. Review monthly and perform a deeper quarterly strategy review.
