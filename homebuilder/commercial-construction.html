SERVICE GUIDE

Understanding Commercial Construction

Commercial projects require careful planning of the use of the building, approvals, structural requirements, services, access, fire and safety considerations where applicable, project schedule and budget. The earlier these requirements are coordinated, the less likely major changes are to occur during construction.

How to approach this service

Before work starts, ask for the drawings, scope of work, material standards, exclusions, payment stages, responsibilities and quality-control expectations to be documented. This gives a non-technical client a clear reference point throughout the project.

What affects the cost?

Cost is influenced by covered area, design complexity, site conditions, structural requirements, material specifications, labour, access, finishing level and changing market prices. For that reason, a current project quotation should be based on the actual scope rather than a generic rate.

Important: Construction prices can change in Pakistan. Any rate shown by SMA Builders should be treated according to its stated date, scope and validity period, and a final quotation should be prepared for the specific project.

Need help understanding the scope?

Share your property type, location, plot size, covered area if known and the stage you are at. We can help you identify the information needed before comparing quotations.

Commercial Construction FAQs

These answers are written for property owners who may not have a technical construction background.

Commercial buildings can have different structural loads, services, circulation, parking, fire-safety requirements, approvals and operating needs. The scope should therefore be developed around the actual building use rather than applying a residential construction checklist.

Compare the same drawings and scope. Check structural work, MEP services, finishes, external works, approvals or exclusions, payment stages, project duration, variation rules and the standards of materials. A cheaper headline rate is not meaningful if important work is missing from the scope.
