# SMA Builders — Single Services Hub SEO/AI Strategy

The website now uses one primary `/services.html` page rather than separate service directories. Each service has a descriptive section with a stable anchor, unique visible copy, a relevant image, and contextual internal links.

## Principles
- People-first content and clear navigation.
- Direct explanations suitable for non-technical property owners.
- Service names and related concepts appear in prominent headings and descriptive links.
- Images sit next to relevant service text and use descriptive alt text.
- Structured data describes the visible page; it does not claim services that are not shown.
- Avoid keyword stuffing and mass-generated thin pages.
- Real SMA Builders project photography should replace representative images before launch.
